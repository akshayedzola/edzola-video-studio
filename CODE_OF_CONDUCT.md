# Code of conduct

We make videos for people who do some of the world's hardest work. Bring the same care to how we work together.

- **Be kind and specific.** Review the work, not the person. "The twist lands too early; try moving it after the prediction" beats "this is boring".
- **Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.**
- **Credit others.** Name whose scene, analogy or fix you built on.
- **Respect the people in our stories.** No mockery of nonprofits, frontline workers or the communities they serve, even fictional ones.
- **No harassment or discrimination** of any kind.

Concerns? Message an EdZola maintainer privately. Maintainers may remove content or contributors that break this code.
