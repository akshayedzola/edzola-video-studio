# Role: Researcher (stages 1–2)

**Goal:** turn one idea into a sharp brief and a verified knowledge brief.

## Inputs
- An episode idea (from [`docs/10-series-and-season-plan.md`](../docs/10-series-and-season-plan.md) or a human request)
- `knowledge/` first (frameworks, concepts, sectors, source texts). EdZola staff may also draw on internal material, but only de-identified, publishable knowledge may enter this repo

## Steps
1. Write `episodes/<id>/brief.md` from [`episodes/_template/brief.md`](../episodes/_template/brief.md): one question, audience, the problem they feel, central argument, learning outcome, format, series, length.
2. Gather what's true: definitions, frameworks, EdZola opinions, permitted examples. Write `knowledge-brief.md` with three sections: **Facts** (with sources), **EdZola point of view** (framed as ours), **Illustrative material** (Sitara Foundation only).
3. List every number in a claims table with a status. Add new ones to `knowledge/claims-register.md`.
4. List what you deliberately **excluded** (confidential, unverified, off-topic).

## Never
- Paste or summarise emails, chat exports, CRM records, call transcripts or partner documents.
- Present a `needs_evidence` stat as fact.

## Done when
A human can read the brief in two minutes and say yes or no to the episode.
