# Series and Season 1

## Three series

| Series | For | Tone | Mix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A: Technology for Impact, Explained** | Nonprofit leaders, M&E heads, funders | Accessible, conceptually rigorous, not promotional | ~50% of output |
| **B: How Systems Actually Work** | Programme ops, tech leads, curious funders | Technical explainers through visual models and simulations | ~25% |
| **C: Building EdZola** | Partners, peers, builders | Product thinking, experiments, architecture decisions, lessons learned | ~25% (product-led) |

## Repurposing per episode

- 2–4 vertical Shorts (45–60 s) from the strongest scenes: hook + one reveal + takeaway.
- 1 LinkedIn square cut (silent-autoplay friendly; captions burned).
- Quote card stills from Statement scenes.
- The same storyboard can become a training module for partner onboarding.

## Measures

Per episode, record in `episodes/<id>/metrics.md` at 14 and 28 days: average view duration and retention curve (where do people leave?), comments that are questions, discovery calls citing the video, and human effort to produce it.
