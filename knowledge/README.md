# Knowledge base

Verified, publishable EdZola knowledge that episodes draw on. This is the **what we teach** layer. Storyboards are the **how we teach it** layer, and each must list the files it uses in its `sources` field.

## Map

| Folder | What lives here |
|---|---|
| [edzola/](edzola/) | Who EdZola is, brand voice and terminology. **Read before writing a single line of narration.** |
| [frameworks/](frameworks/) | MAP (Model · Align · Power with AI), its glossary, entity library and dashboard guidance; Technology That Sticks; the three operating maps |
| [concepts/](concepts/) | 15 EdZola points of view, each a candidate episode thesis |
| [sectors/](sectors/) | 16 sectors (education, health, livelihoods…), each with its data thread, entities and 4 lifecycle stages |
| [source-texts/](source-texts/) | Published long-form source material (the seven Field Essays) |
| [products/](products/) | Public descriptions of ImpactOS, Zolabs, DozAI and others |
| [case-studies/](case-studies/) | The recurring **fictional** cast (Sitara Foundation). Real case studies need written partner consent |
| [claims-register.md](claims-register.md) | Every factual claim we may say on screen, and whether it can be published |

## Rules for this folder

1. **Publishable only.** No beneficiary, donor or partner-staff names, no phone numbers, no partner-confidential data, no call recordings or transcripts. Fictional examples are labelled fictional.
2. **Separate fact from opinion.** Mark claims as `verified` (has evidence), `edzola_opinion` (our point of view, said as ours), `needs_evidence` (do not publish until checked) or `illustrative`.
3. **Numbers go through the claims register.** Every statistic in a video must be in [claims-register.md](claims-register.md).
4. **Improve it.** Found a better explanation, a clearer definition, a gap? Add it in a PR (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md)). Changes to `edzola/`, `frameworks/` and `products/` need review from the EdZola content owners (see `.github/CODEOWNERS`).

## Where else knowledge comes from

EdZola staff may draw on EdZola's internal playbook, but anything added here must be rewritten as publishable, de-identified knowledge. **Never paste internal documents, emails, chat exports, partner data or meeting transcripts into this repository.**
