## Voice reminders

- Problem first. Open with the viewer's frustration before any EdZola mention.
- Avoid the "it's not X, it's Y" construction in narration. Use a myth/reality *visual* instead and let the voice say "Many teams think X. But Y."
- No em dashes in narration (brand founder-voice rule; the validator warns).
