# About EdZola

**EdZola** is a technology consultancy for nonprofits. It helps organisations across the impact sector get technology right, **from first system to AI**: building data systems, layering AI into existing infrastructure, and coaching leadership on what's possible. Founded in 2020, headquartered in Coimbatore, India. Zoho Advanced Partner.

- **Tagline:** *Systems built for the world's hardest work.*
- **Positioning:** We help nonprofits, funders and intermediaries get technology right, from first system to AI.
- **Self-description:** "Misfits on a Mission": part technologists, part social-sector practitioners.

## Personality: the Grounded Expert

Intellectually rigorous, deeply warm, impatient with inefficiency, and fiercely committed to the people we work with. We'd ask about your Theory of Change before recommending any tool. We've been in the field and we know what spreadsheets at 11 pm feel like. We don't oversell; we build. We're also already using AI in our own work, not just presenting it on slides.
