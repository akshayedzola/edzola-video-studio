# Brand voice for narration

Applies to every script, caption, title and description. The validator enforces the mechanical parts; humans own the rest.

## Voice principles

1. **Problem first, solution second.** Open with the viewer's pain before mentioning EdZola.
2. **Plain language.** If a sector outsider wouldn't understand a word, replace it.
3. **Active voice, short sentences.** Declarative and confident.
4. **Validate, don't lecture.** "Sound familiar?" before prescribing anything.
5. **Concrete beats abstract.** "The same learner in three spreadsheets", not "data fragmentation challenges".
6. **Warm but not casual.** A trusted colleague, not a salesperson, and not a friend texting.

## Writing for the ear

- One beat = one spoken sentence, ideally under 15 words.
- Contractions, "you" voice, British English (programme, organisation, judgement).
- **No em dashes** in narration. Use full stops; the voice pauses better on them.
- Avoid "It's not X, it's Y." Say "Many teams think X. But Y." and let the visual do the contrast.
- Opinions framed as ours: "We've learned…", "In our experience…".

## Terminology

| Use | Not |
|---|---|
| nonprofits | NGOs, charities |
| programmes | programs |
| organisations | organizations |
| partners | clients, customers |
| impact sector | development sector |
| LMICs (spell out first: low- and middle-income countries) | developing countries |
| field teams / field staff | on-ground workers |
| go-live | launch date |
| ImpactOS (always capitalised) | Impact OS, impactos |

**Never use:** disrupt, unlock, unleash, supercharge, leverage, growth hacking, scale fast, "digital transformation journey", "AI-powered" as a blanket label, "world-class", "innovative solutions", "cutting-edge AI".

## Phrases that work

- "Sound familiar?"
- "Your [X] deserves better than a spreadsheet."
- "We don't leave until your team is actually using it."
- "From first system to AI."
- "Where you start depends on where you are."
- "Your data lives in spreadsheets, WhatsApp and someone's head."

## Anti-patterns

| Instead of | Do |
|---|---|
| "We are pleased to announce…" | Lead with what it does and for whom |
| "Our innovative platform…" | Name the specific problem it solves |
| "AI can transform your organisation" | "Here's what AI looks like in your workflow, specifically" |
| Long feature lists | One outcome + one or two enabling features + one proof point |
