# Three operating maps: people, money, evidence

**Status:** edzola_opinion
**Sources:** EdZola's "How nonprofits operate" curriculum (internal; summarised here).

A nonprofit runs on three flows that meet at accountability:

- **People flow:** how beneficiaries, staff and volunteers move through the programme.
- **Money flow:** how funds arrive, are restricted, spent and reported.
- **Evidence flow:** how activity becomes data, data becomes reports, and reports become decisions.

The question to ask any organisation: *"Show me how the people move, how the money moves, and how the evidence moves."*

## Why it matters for video

This is the natural spine for the **Reveal the invisible system** format. Animate one beneficiary (people), one grant (money) and one report (evidence) moving through the same organisation, then show where they collide. The future `DataJourney` template is designed for exactly this.
